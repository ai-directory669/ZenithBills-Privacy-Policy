Zero Personal Data Collection ZenithBills **does NOT collect, harvest, store on external servers, or transmit** any of your personal or financial data. - We do NOT require an account or registration. - We do NOT collect your name, phone number, address, or email address. - We do NOT track your device location. --- ### 2. No Access to Financial Credentials ZenithBills does NOT connect to your bank accounts, credit card portals, or financial institutions.
