Thank you for choosing ZenithBills ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. ZenithBills is built from the ground up as a privacy-first, offline-ready utility.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

ZenithBills does NOT collect, harvest, store on external servers, or transmit any of your personal or financial data.

  • We do NOT require an account or registration.
  • We do NOT collect your name, phone number, address, or email address.
  • We do NOT track your device location or GPS.

2. Local-Only Storage (On Your Device)

All data entered into ZenithBills (including subscription names, billing amounts, due dates, categories, payment notes, and reminder settings) is stored exclusively on your local device using a local encrypted SQLite database.

Your financial records never leave your phone and are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party advertising brokers.

3. No Access to Financial Credentials

ZenithBills does NOT connect to your bank accounts, credit card portals, or financial institutions. You never enter banking passwords, credit card numbers, or social security numbers into the app.

4. Device Permissions

ZenithBills operates with the principle of least privilege:

  • No Camera / Microphone Access: We do not request or use audio/video permissions.
  • No Contacts Access: We do not access your contact lists.
  • No Location Access: We do not access location telemetry.
  • Internet Permission: Used solely for standard platform in-app purchasing verification.

5. Third-Party Services & Purchases

If you purchase or subscribe to Pro features, transactions are processed directly by the platform store (e.g., Amazon Appstore) through their secure native payment systems. ZenithBills never handles, processes, or stores your credit card details.

6. Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA Rights)

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you maintain complete ownership of your data:

  • You can export your entire subscription list to a CSV file at any time via Settings > Export All Data.
  • You can permanently wipe all records from your device anytime via Settings > Clear All Data.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all stored data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

ZenithBills is a general utility app for tracking personal expenses. It does not target, collect, or process information from children under the age of 13.
